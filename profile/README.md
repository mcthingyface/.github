# McThingyface

McThingyface Limited is a small UK company making apps for Apple TV, iPhone and iPad.

## Watchy

**Watchy** brings your Jellyfin, Plex, Emby and SMB films and series into one library on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad.

- Continue Watching from every source, including on the Apple TV Top Shelf
- The next episode with one press
- Pick for Us when you can't decide what to watch
- Rule-based collections across all your libraries
- Intro, recap and credit skipping with Jellyfin

It comes with a 7-day free trial, then a £4.99 one-time unlock that covers Apple TV, iPhone and iPad. There's no subscription.
